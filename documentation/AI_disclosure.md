# AI assistance and contribution disclosure

This disclosure records the known preparation history of this pilot project. The human author should review it for completeness and accuracy before any external submission.

- ChatGPT was used to inspect the supplied source files, check consistency between reported metrics and the supplied data/code, verify bibliographic metadata against publication records, draft and format the manuscript, generate figures, and prepare supporting documentation.
- The catalog and query records are synthetic. No real customer conversations, personal data, or scraped retailer data are used.
- No human participants were recruited, and no human-subject evaluation was conducted.
- AI tools assisted with code, data generation, analysis support, and manuscript preparation. They are not listed as authors.
- The human author remains responsible for reviewing the code, running the experiment, verifying references and results, and ensuring that the final manuscript and disclosure meet any relevant submission requirements.

This project is an exploratory synthetic pilot report, not a peer-reviewed publication.
